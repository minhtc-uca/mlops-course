# Final exam (MCQ)

The final exam is an **individual multiple-choice exam**. It counts for
**50% of the course grade**. The other 50% is the team project, which includes
your Week 1–4 lab reports.

- **Format:** multiple choice, each question has **exactly one** correct answer.
- **Weight:** 50% of the course grade, individual.
- **Scope:** lectures and labs from Weeks 1–4.
- **Date, time, room and duration:** announced by the school.
- **Keywords to focus on:** [Week 5 deck, slide 7](#what-to-revise).
